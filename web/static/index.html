What the metrics mean

ROI: net profit / invested capital over the period. Annualized ROI: equivalent per-year return (IRR), comparable across terms.

NPV: present value of the cash flow discounted at the hurdle rate. NPV > 0 adds value beyond the opportunity cost.

IRR: per-year rate that zeroes the NPV. Desirable when > hurdle.

Payback: month when redemption recovers the capital (simple/discounted). "n/r" = term does not recover.

PI (profitability index): NPV/capital + 1. Above 1 pays the opportunity cost.

Alpha: annualized return − hurdle. Hurdle line: what the same deposit flow would earn at the minimum rate.
